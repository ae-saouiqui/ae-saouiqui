# Hi, I'm Amine Es-saouiqui

### Machine Learning Engineer | AI & GenAI Specialist | MLOps Enthusiast

Welcome to my GitHub! I'm an engineering student at **ENSA Al Hoceima**, specializing in Digital Transformation and Artificial Intelligence. 

I am passionate about building and understanding end-to-end AI systems—from data processing and model training to deployment and production-oriented workflows. My focus lies at the intersection of **Machine Learning Engineering, Generative AI (RAG/LLMs), and MLOps**.

Currently, I am exploring **Kubernetes, CI/CD, and distributed training (FSDP)**, while actively contributing to research-oriented projects in Deep Learning and Representation Learning.

---

## 🚀 What I Work On

- **GenAI & RAG:** Building robust Retrieval-Augmented Generation pipelines, semantic search, and LLM/VLM applications.
- **ML Engineering & MLOps:** Designing reproducible training pipelines, model serving (FastAPI), and workflow orchestration (Airflow, Docker).
- **Representation Learning:** Contrastive learning, dense/sparse vector representations, and efficient fine-tuning (LoRA).
- **Data Engineering:** Building scalable ETL/ELT pipelines, Medallion architectures, and data warehousing.
